Taskbar version

Two ways to live in the taskbar. Buttons start a Claude session / make it need you. Hover the bar item to open.

T1

Black capsule in the taskbar

The same black shape as the top notch, sitting in the empty part of your taskbar next to the tray. Hover: it grows upward into the panel with the same spring. A working session buds out as a pod to its left; "needs you" turns it amber with a soft glow. Matches the top notch exactly, so switching between Top and Taskbar feels like the same thing.

T2

Native tray item + Windows flyout

No black shape: the rings and numbers sit right on the taskbar like a Windows tray item (same hover highlight as the volume/Wi-Fi icons), and it follows a light or dark taskbar. Click opens a Windows-style flyout above the taskbar, like the clock or volume panel. "Needs you" shows as a small amber dot. Quieter and more native, but looks different from the top notch.
