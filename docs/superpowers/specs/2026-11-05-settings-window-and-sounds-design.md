# Settings window, alert sounds and banners that stay

Date: 2026-11-05. Approved by the owner on 2026-11-05.

## Why

Settings in the notch panel have outgrown it (display, Claude source, task alerts, prayer,
alert levels, version), and sounds would add more. The owner also wants to *hear* alerts, not
only see them: a bell when Claude needs you or a prayer is near, and their own azan at the
prayer time. A prayer banner that vanishes after a minute is easy to miss, so it should stay
like the needs-you bell until the owner looks.

## Goals

1. **Settings window.** The gear in the panel, and a new **Settings…** item in the tray menu,
   open one separate window, "Claude Notch settings". Opening it again focuses the open one.
2. **Sounds.** Built-in bell variants (no bundled azan), plus the owner's own sound files.
3. **Banners that stay.** The prayer banner stays in the closed notch until the owner opens
   the notch, like the needs-you bell.

## Non-goals

- No bundled azan recording for now (licensing of found recordings is uncertain). The owner
  adds their own file.
- No volume slider: sounds follow the Windows volume.
- No sound for "Also when a task finishes" or for other notices; only needs-you and prayer.
- No change to the toast texts, prayer data, or usage reading.

## Anti-goals (failure even if it "works")

- Two sounds at once (Windows' toast sound on top of ours, or bell over azan).
- A nonstop animation in the notch (each costs ~9 % of a core; see the redesign notes).
- Settings that no longer save as you change them, or the notch not updating live.
- Losing the user's sound file when they move or delete the original.

## Approaches considered

**Settings home** (owner picked from three sketches): (a) a list of group cards inside the
panel, (b) **a separate settings window** — picked, (c) tabs inside the panel. The window gives
room for every group without scrolling the notch, and opens even while the notch is hidden.

**Playing sound** (owner handed the how to the builder): (1) **an HTML `<audio>` element in the
notch's webview** — picked: no new dependency, smallest change; risk: WebView2's autoplay
policy may block sound before a user click, so verify it first and fix with the WebView2
`--autoplay-policy=no-user-gesture-required` browser argument if needed; (2) the `rodio` crate
in Rust — always plays, but a new dependency and a larger installer; (3) Windows MCI — no
dependency, but an old API with thread affinity.

## The settings window

- One Tauri window, label `settings`, about 640 × 460 logical px, centred on the notch's
  screen, normal title bar with ✕, listed in the taskbar while open, dark like the notch
  (same tokens and Manrope font). Closing it just closes it; the app keeps running.
- Left column: group list. Right side: the chosen group's settings. Groups and contents:
  - **Display:** Show in, Show on, Position, Stay behind windows.
  - **Claude:** Get usage from, Start WSL if it's stopped, Show when Claude needs you, Also
    when a task finishes.
  - **Prayer:** Prayer times, Zone, Remind before.
  - **Sounds:** Prayer sound, Claude needs you (below).
  - **Alert levels:** Session alert at, Week alert at.
  - **About:** Version x.y.z, Check for updates / Update to x.y.z (same behaviour as today).
- Saves as you change it, as today (`save_settings`); the notch updates live through the
  existing `usage-updated` event.
- The panel loses its settings view. Its gear opens the window; the "Update to x.y.z" link at
  the top of the panel stays.
- Keyboard: every control reachable by Tab, visible focus, Esc closes the window. WCAG AA
  contrast as in the notch.

## Sounds

Three rows in the Sounds group, each sound row with a **Play** button to preview:

- **Bell:** which built-in bell (four variants). Default: the first.
- **Claude needs you:** **Bell**, **My file** or **Off**. Default: Bell.
- **Prayer:** **Bell**, **My azan** or **Off**. Default: Bell. With My azan, the prayer *time*
  plays the azan; the reminder before it always plays the bell (unless Prayer is Off).
**Choose file** stores a copy in the app's folder (`sounds/needs.<ext>`,
  `sounds/azan.<ext>`), replacing any earlier one. Accept mp3, wav, ogg, m4a up to 10 MB;
  anything else gets a clear message and nothing is saved. A **Remove** link deletes the copy
  and falls back to the default.

**Built-in bells:** four short synthesized chimes (about 1–2 s), generated by a script in
`tools/` with ffmpeg and committed as small `.ogg` files under `src/sounds/`, so there is no
licence question. Working names: Chime (two notes), Ding (one bright note), Soft (low, mellow),
Double (two quick dings).

**When sounds play:**

| Moment | Sound |
|---|---|
| A session starts needing you (attention goes up) | the needs-you sound |
| Prayer reminder (Remind before) | the bell |
| Prayer time | the azan if added, else the bell |
| Task finished, other notices | none |

**Priority:** the prayer sound wins. If the azan (or the prayer-time bell) is playing, a bell
due at the same time is skipped; if a bell is playing when the prayer time comes, it stops and
the prayer sound plays. Only one sound plays at a time.

**Stopping:** opening the notch (hover-open or click) stops whatever is playing. A plain
pass-over that does not open the notch does not stop it.

**Windows notifications** stay, but are made silent whenever the notch plays its own sound for
that alert, so the owner never hears two sounds. If the notification plugin cannot make a
silent toast on Windows, the builder reports it back instead of shipping double sounds.

## Banners that stay

Like the needs-you bell (owner: "when hover gone the bell, the prayer time also like that"):

- A prayer banner (reminder or time) shows in full for its first minute, as today, then
  collapses to a small moon icon beside the usage numbers, the way the orange bell sits there.
- The moon stays until the notch is opened (hover-open or click), or a newer banner replaces it.
  Opening the notch clears it and stops any sound.
- A banner that fired before the page listened (the start-up case already handled by
  `LAST_BANNER`) still shows, then collapses the same way.
- Session notices (the 6 s flash) are unchanged.

## Constraints

- Tauri v2, plain HTML/CSS/JS in `src/`, Rust in `src-tauri/src`; builds from WSL through
  PowerShell (README "Build from source").
- The notch window stays a fixed-size, click-through window; the settings window is a normal one.
- Capabilities: the new window needs the same `core:default` permissions and the commands the
  settings use; CSP must allow playing the sound source chosen (asset protocol or blob URLs).
- Ponytail: reuse the existing settings code and styles; no framework.

## Left to the builder

- Whether the settings window reuses `notch.css` tokens via a shared file or a copy of the tokens.
- How the webview gets user sound files (Tauri asset protocol vs. bytes through a command).
- Exact bell synthesis parameters and names, as long as there are four distinct, pleasant ones.
- How the settings window and the notch share settings code (move it into `settings.js`).
- The acceptance checklist steps for the README.

## Builder's calls (marked for the owner to check)

- "Settings…" also in the tray menu.
- One Bell row shared by needs-you and prayer (one less setting).
- Sounds play even while the notch is hidden or a full-screen app is open.
- Opening, not hovering past, stops a sound.
- Max file size 10 MB.
